TaskVibe FAQ

How do I add a new task?

Enter the task description in the input field at the top, optionally add a comment, date, and time, and click "Add Task" or press Enter.

How can I mark a task as completed?

Click the checkbox next to the task to mark it as completed. Click again to mark it as active.

What do the filter buttons do?

The "All" button shows all tasks, "Active" shows only incomplete tasks, and "Completed" shows only completed tasks.

How do I switch between light and dark themes?

Click the plane toggle button in the top-right corner to switch between light and dark themes.

Are my tasks saved automatically?

Yes, tasks are saved to your browser's local storage and persist across page reloads.

What happens when a task's due date and time are reached?

Tasks with a set due date and time are automatically deleted once the specified time is reached.
